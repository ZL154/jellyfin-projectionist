# Projectionist v1.3.0

**Jellyfin 12 support, and a fix for the blank web UI on Jellyfin 10.11.11+ and 12.**

This release also includes everything from v1.2.0, which was never published to the catalog.

## Which file do I need?

| Your Jellyfin | Download |
|---------------|----------|
| 12.x | `projectionist_1.3.0.1.zip` |
| 10.11.x | `projectionist_1.3.0.0.zip` |

If you install from the plugin catalog, Jellyfin picks the right one for you.

## Fixed

- **Web UI broken after enabling the plugin ("failed to decode")** on Jellyfin 10.11.11+ and 12 (#7). Those versions compress `index.html`, and Projectionist was appending its script tag to the compressed bytes. Thanks @nicholaichaney-cpu, @Mannycachorron and @grainsoflight for the reports.
- Skip-rate reports and the admin preview no longer fail with 401 on Jellyfin 12.
- Skip-button settings (delay, on/off) and preload mode now apply even if the web client was first opened on the login screen.
- The skip button no longer reappears over the episode after you skip a preroll.
- Playing an episode from a season list now starts at the episode you picked, not the season opener.
- Native TV and mobile apps no longer flash a "Playback Error" before episodes.
- Skip statistics and post-rolls work for regular (non-admin) users; they were getting 403.
- Preroll clips no longer get matched online to unrelated films (wrong title and poster); clips already matched are reset.
- New accounts no longer see the "Projectionist Prerolls" library until a restart.

## New

- **Episode prerolls in Android TV, iOS, Roku and other native apps.** Turn on *Episodes in native apps* in the plugin settings. The preroll is put inside the episode's stream on the server, converted to the episode's own format; subtitles, resume points and intro-skip markers are kept in sync. It's off by default: the "Always" option plays those episodes as an HLS remux instead of direct play, for `.mkv` files (the type Jellyfin can remux cleanly); other files get a preroll whenever Jellyfin streams them as HLS anyway.
- **Post-rolls now play** (they were listed in v1.2.0 but only logged). After a movie, after an episode with auto-play off, or after the last episode of a series.
- Jellyfin 12 support (separate .NET 10 build).
- Episode prerolls work on modern Jellyfin Web, including 12.
- When the next episode auto-plays, it gets its own preroll.
- Opt out a whole series with one entry.
- "Browse Library…" picker for opt-outs, and opt-outs are listed by name.

## From v1.2.0

- Genre-aware library rules.
- Per-item opt-out ("never preroll this").
- Skip-rate analytics per preroll.
- Coming-soon trailer from a movie you haven't watched.
- Loudness check (ffmpeg `volumedetect`).
- European ratings for the maturity gate: FSK, CSA, ACB, Medierådet, ICAA, Eirin.
- Assorted fixes: thread-safe selection, bounded cooldown store, discovery skips hidden folders, schedule rules warn about bad dates.
